Two help articles each call their currency “the in-game currency.” Read together, they describe a pair: Stars are what you spend on the island’s goods, and Gems are what the mobile articles tell you to buy with real money, then spend on Stars.

![Showcase room with a ride and a pet, from the Steam store page](/images/official/steam/ss-01.jpg)

## Stars

From “What are Stars?”:

- Stars buy costumes, rides, furniture, and more.
- Sources named in the article: minigames, fishing, daily missions, ad rewards, and the Shop.
- The balance is at the **top right** of the screen.
- “How to purchase more Stars?” says the Shop purchase uses **Gems**, and that the game is free to play.

Fishing and minigames are the two loops with their own pages. Daily missions sit on the Phone with the rest of the mission list. The article mentions ad rewards as a Stars source and does not describe an ad placement. If your build shows one, it is optional.

## Gems

From “How to buy more Gems?”:

- Gems are described as an in-game currency for speeding progress.
- The purchase path in that article is **Google Play** or the **Apple App Store**.

<Callout kind="note" title="Steam purchases are a different sentence">
The Steam store page lists in-app purchases and does not quote a Gem pack price in the text checked on 22 October 2026. The mobile-store names in the help article may not match the Steam shop button. Buy from the client you are logged into, and treat a pack price on a fan page as unverified.
</Callout>

The hub guide adds a free source: **Achievements** on the Phone pay Gems. Every achievement has five stages, shown as stamps, and later stages are harder. That track is the in-game one. Steam also lists a Steam Achievements category. This guide does not merge the two lists or invent achievement names.

## Other items people call money

| Item | What a checked source actually says |
| --- | --- |
| Fish, insects, ores, fossils | Steam says you can sell them or display them. No unit price is printed. |
| Upgradites | A reward line on official coupon notice 8329 (500 Upgradites). That coupon’s window closed on 5 August 2026. The notice does not define the item. |
| Coupon Gems | Notice 8329 included 100 Gems. Current reported codes are on [Codes](/codes), and they expire. |
| Pet combination currency | The hub says the cost depends on grade and does not print the number. |

Spending Stars on a costume or a ride is the loop the Stars article is written for. A house full of furniture is the other sink, on [Homes](/homes). Part-time jobs pay “rewards” in the quest article and do not print a Star amount. See [Jobs](/jobs).
