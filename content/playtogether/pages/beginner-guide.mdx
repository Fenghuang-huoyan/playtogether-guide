HAEGIN’s short description of the game is the whole pitch: an imaginary town where you play and connect with friends. Steam’s page adds the download count line (“over 200 million downloads”), home parties, minigames, fishing, insects, fossils, and pets.

![Character creator on the Steam store page](/images/official/steam/ss-00.jpg)

## The two buttons that matter

| Control | What the help pages say |
| --- | --- |
| Phone, top right | Minigames, home parties, customization, recycling, missions, map, friends, collection book, inbox, settings, account link. |
| Bag, top right | Rides, pets, tools, fish, food, ingredients, recycled items. Tools include the Fishing Rod and the Bug Net. |

Settings on the Phone cover sound, vibration, invites, language, notifications, frame rate, resolution, account linking, and the support menu. That is also where coupon entry lives. Steps are on [Codes](/codes).

## Plaza landmarks

These are the places the English help articles and the official hub guide name. They are a first-hour route, not every NPC.

| Place | Where the source puts it | Why you go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fishing Shop | Plaza, near the docks | Buy a Fishing Rod. The Shop sells rods too. |
| Pet Shop | Plaza | Eggs, food, beds, toys. |
| Game Center | Plaza | Game Party, also under Phone, Play Game. |
| Flight Attendant | Plaza | Travel between regions. The return home is free. |
| Camping Ground | Plaza, opposite the beach, between the School and the Ghost House | Camping, part-time jobs, challenges, Grossard’s Bug Nets, pet combination NPC. |
| Captain Jack | Plaza, in the hub guide | Forgotten Island. See [Events](/events). |

## A sensible first hour

1. Finish the character creator. Steam’s feature list calls out skin tones, hairstyles, body types, and costumes.
2. Open the Phone map and walk the Plaza until the Fishing Shop, Pet Shop, and Camping Ground are pins you recognize.
3. Pick one money loop and one social loop. A rod or a net is the money loop. A friend Follow, a home visit, or a Game Party is the social loop. Stars are explained on [Money](/money).
4. If you want a pet, the Pet Shop is the official shop. Growth rules are on [Pets](/pets). A 2021 fan guide’s free-egg mission is not repeated here as a current reward.
5. Redeem a coupon only after you can see Enter Coupon. Then open the mailbox.

## Travel

The hub guide calls travel a move between regions, started by talking to the Flight Attendant and choosing Travel. The regions it names are East Asia, Southeast Asia, Europe, America, and Vietnam. Each visit stamps a passport under Profile, then the plane icon. Your home region shows a house icon. Returning home costs nothing. You leave the same way you arrived.

The store page also discloses generative AI for some development assets and for NPC conversation, with a review step HAEGIN describes. That disclosure is on the store page if you want the full wording.

Friends, once added, can Follow you or be Summoned from Phone, Friends. Summon asks them to come to you. Follow moves you to them. You can also tap a character and choose Follow. The in-game achievement track, separate from whatever Steam Achievements the client exposes, is a five-stage stamp track on the Phone that pays Gems. Names of individual Steam achievements are not copied here.
