## How to redeem

There is no redeem box on this website. Codes are entered in the game.

1. Finish the opening if the coupon button is missing. MobileMatters says redeem opens after the tutorial. HAEGIN’s notices say that a failed redeem is often fixed by closing the client completely and launching it again.
2. On Android and Steam, tap the **Phone** icon, then **Settings**, then **Enter Coupon**. Type the code and tap **Claim Reward**. UrGameTips names the confirm button **Receive Gift**. The label in your build is the one that matters.
3. On iOS, open the coupon page HAEGIN linked in notices 8326 and 8329: [haegin.kr/playtogether/coupon](http://haegin.kr/playtogether/coupon).
4. Open the **Inbox** on the Phone and take the reward before the mailbox storage period ends. Official notices say an unclaimed mailbox reward can be removed for good.
5. One redemption per account. Paste the code. A monthly string is easy to mistype.

## When a code fails

- **Already redeemed.** The string can still look “active” on a tracker for someone else.
- **Expired.** Campaign coupons die on a clock the trackers hear about late. The Teenieping pair above had a printed end time, and that time has passed.
- **Wrong client.** iOS uses the web coupon page in the official notices. Android and Steam use the Phone.
- **Mailbox left unopened.** A successful redeem still has to be collected.
