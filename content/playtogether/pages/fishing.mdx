Fishing is a collection loop with a shop at the end of it. The help center’s rules are short. The species list is long, and it lives in the client.

![Pier and ocean on the Steam store page](/images/official/steam/ss-05.jpg)

## Cast, in the order the help article gives

1. Equip a Fishing Rod: **Bag → Tool → Fishing Rod**.
2. Stand where there is water. Shallow water and empty ground do not count. Being in the water, or jumping, blocks the cast.
3. Use the rod. With it equipped you can select and buy bait. The article says the bait you have equipped changes the probability of certain fish.
4. Bait you do not buy can be crafted at the **Kaia Workshop**.
5. A **Boat** reaches fishing at the farthest part of the sea.

After the catch, the “What is Fishing?” article gives two uses: sell the fish, or put it in a Fish Tank. Stars from fishing are named on the Stars article. See [Money](/money). Prices per species are not in these articles.

## What changes the fish

| Factor | Source | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spot | Steam store text | Pond, sea, and swimming pool each have fish that do not appear in the other spots. |
| Far sea | “How can I Fish?” | The boat is how you fish the far reaches. |
| Time of day | “What is Fishing?” | Day or night changes the probability, along with location and timing. |
| Bait | “How can I Fish?” | Equipped bait changes which fish are likely. |
| Collection | Steam store text | The Illustrated Book tracks the set. New species are added over time. The store count is over 600. |

<Callout kind="warn" title="This is not a species spreadsheet">
No English help article checked on 22 October 2026 prints a fish name, a sell price, or a shadow size. Copying a fan table from an older mobile patch would invent a list this client may already have replaced. Open the Illustrated Book for the names, and trust the spot you are standing in.
</Callout>

## Where to start

The Fishing Shop is in the Plaza near the docks. If the pin is missing, use Phone, then Map, the same fallback the Camping Ground article gives. Rods are also sold at the Shop. The store screenshot above is the pier art from the Steam page. It shows the kind of water the feature is selling. It is not a labeled map of every node.

Home visits are a second place to fish. Steam’s home-party paragraph says you can catch fish at a friend’s house. The house rules for whose rod and whose tank apply are not spelled out in the articles used here. Try it in a party, then read [Homes](/homes).
