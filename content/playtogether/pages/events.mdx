Play Together’s events are campaigns with an end date, plus a few repeating activities the hub guide describes. A static wiki is a bad calendar. It is a decent record of which doors are still worth knocking on.

![Resort pool on the Steam store page](/images/official/steam/ss-02.jpg)

## Forgotten Island

The hub page “What’s the Play Together” describes Forgotten Island like this:

- It is a special area, and the page says it is accessible on the weekend.
- Talk to **Captain Jack** in the Plaza to go there.
- Treasures are hidden on the island.
- You dig them up with items meant for digging.

<Callout kind="warn" title="The hub page does not show a revision date">
Weekend access and Captain Jack’s spot are quoted from that guide. If the Plaza no longer has the NPC, the rotation has moved. Phone, then Map, is the check. This page will not invent a treasure table.
</Callout>

Steam’s fossil-and-ore sentence is a separate claim about the wider world. It does not say those nodes are Forgotten Island. Digging items on the island and a Bug Net in the Camping Ground are different tools.

## Coupons are the event most people mean

When a campaign hits a goal, HAEGIN often pays with a coupon notice rather than an automatic gift. The Teenieping pair is the clear 2026 example:

| Code | Notice | Window | Reward the notice printed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PTTEENIEPINGTY | 8326 | Until 5 Aug 2026, 14:59 UTC | Theme Draw Ticket, Lucky Gem Box, Lucky Star Box, two Premium Card Packs |
| PTTEENIEPINGTYSM | 8329 | Same end time | 100 Gems, Lucky Gem Box, Super Premium Card Pack, 500 Upgradites, Magic Pet Accessory Potion |

Both windows are closed. Redeem steps and newer reported strings are on [Codes](/codes).

## An event that already ended

Notice 8147, Better Together, asked players to post a screenshot of at least two people on the official Discord, with an in-game username. The period in the notice is after the 14 May 2026 announcement through 24 May 2026, 11:59 PM KST. Draw prizes and milestone coupons were part of that post. The dates are past. Do not hunt that channel for a May reward in October.

## What keeps running between campaigns

These are systems, not limited events, and they have their own pages:

- Game Party and the other minigames named on Steam. See [Minigames](/minigames).
- Home parties. See [Homes](/homes).
- Fishing and insect collecting, which gain species over time according to the store page.
- Limited pets, which the store page says appear only in specific periods. The Pet Shop either has them or it does not.

Check [notice.haegin.kr](https://notice.haegin.kr/) when a loading-screen banner mentions a collaboration. If the notice includes a coupon, copy it from the notice, then confirm the client still accepts it.
