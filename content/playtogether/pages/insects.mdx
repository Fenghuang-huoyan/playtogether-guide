The Steam page treats insects, lizards, ores, and fossils as one boast: over 300 insect species, dinosaur fossils, rare diamonds, and the choice to sell the find or display it. The help center’s “Gather” article is narrower, and it is the one with steps.

![Campsite on the Steam store page](/images/official/steam/ss-03.jpg)

## Catch an insect

1. Buy a **Bug Net** from **Grossard** in the **Camping Ground**, or check the Shop. The Camping Ground is in the Plaza, opposite the beach, between the School and the Ghost House. Phone, then Map, if the path is unclear.
2. Equip it: **Bag → Tool**.
3. Tap the **Bug Net** button on the bottom right.
4. If the insect runs, slow down. The article says some insects flee when you get close, and a slower approach is the fix.
5. Materials sitting on the ground are not a net catch. Walk up to them. They feed crafting.

Dr. Farbe’s part-time job is “help him collect insects.” That is a quest, with a reward the article does not price. Details are on [Jobs](/jobs).

## What the store adds, and what it does not list

| Claim | Where it is written | What this page will not add |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Over 300 insect species | Steam feature text | Individual names, spawn hours, or sell prices |
| Lizards in the same loop | Steam feature text | A separate lizard tool. The Gather article only requires a net for insects |
| Ores, fossils, diamonds | Steam feature text | A dig-site map. The hub guide’s Forgotten Island uses “items meant for digging,” which is a different sentence |
| Sell or display | Steam feature text | A price table |

<Callout kind="note" title="The Illustrated Book is the insect list">
Same rule as fishing. If a fan page names a beetle and a spawn window, check the collection book in your client before you plan a route around it. The articles checked on 22 October 2026 do not contain that table.
</Callout>

Ground materials and insects both sit in the Bag. Fishing catches are a different tool and a different shop. Stars from selling finds are part of the economy on [Money](/money), with no per-item price copied from an old guide.
