“Job” in the English help center means a part-time quest, not a career track with a wage table. The quest article is one screen long. It is still the best official list this check could find.

![Plaza fountain on the Steam store page](/images/official/steam/ss-09.jpg)

## The two named part-time jobs

| NPC | Task | Reward in the article |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dr. Farbe | Collect insects as a part-timer | “Collect rewards.” No amount. |
| Fireman | Collect branches as a part-timer | “Collect rewards.” No amount. |

Insect catching itself is the Bug Net loop on [Insects](/insects). Doing it for Dr. Farbe is the quest. Bring a net before you accept, because the Gather article requires one for any insect catch.

The Camping article adds the place: everyone can camp at the Camping Ground, part-time jobs and challenges there pay rewards, and you can talk to other players. It does not name further employers.

## Where to stand

The Camping Ground is in the Plaza, on the opposite side of the beach, between the School and the Ghost House. Phone, then Map, if the buildings have moved since the article was written.

Grossard, who sells Bug Nets, is also in the Camping Ground. The pet-combination NPC from the hub guide is in the Camping Ground too. Those are vendors and a fusion NPC, not the part-time jobs in the quest article.

## The shrine challenge

The same quest article describes a second activity that is a race, not a shift:

1. An NPC in a black hoodie offers the challenge.
2. The goal it names is the shrine above the clouds.
3. You clear obstacles at the Camping Ground and climb as high as you can.
4. Cancel with the challenge icon on the left of the screen, then cancel quest.

<Callout kind="note" title="Pay is whatever the client shows">
No Star amount, timer, or daily cap is printed for Dr. Farbe or the Fireman in the articles above. Accept the quest, read the reward line on the prompt, and decide there. A copied “best job” ranking from a fan page is not repeated here.
</Callout>

Minigames are the other rewarded activity HAEGIN names, and they are a queue rather than an NPC shift. See [Minigames](/minigames). Daily missions, which the Stars article lists as a Stars source, live on the Phone.
