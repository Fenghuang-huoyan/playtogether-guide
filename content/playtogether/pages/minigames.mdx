Minigames are the competitive half of a social game. The store page lists modes by name. The hub guide explains the queue that ties a lot of them together.

![Go-kart on the Steam store page](/images/official/steam/ss-07.jpg)

## Names on the Steam page

The feature text, quoted as a list rather than a strategy:

| Mode | How Steam describes it |
| --- | --- |
| Game Party | Last one standing from 30 players |
| Zombie Virus | Named as a minigame, no further rule in the paragraph |
| Obby Race | Named as a minigame |
| Tower of Infinity | Named as a minigame |
| Fashion Star Runway | Named as a minigame |
| Snowball Fight | Named as a minigame |
| Sky High | Named as a minigame |
| School set | “An assortment of additional minigames found only in school” |

The hub guide’s Game Party paragraph matches the 30-player line and adds that the set includes races, obstacle races, survival, and more, and that you earn rewards.

## How to queue

Two paths, both from the hub:

1. **Plaza → Game Center**
2. **Phone → Play Game → Game Party**

If the Game Center is not where you expect, open the map on the Phone. Friends can be pulled in with Follow and Summon once you are friends. Those buttons are on [Beginner guide](/beginner-guide).

<Callout kind="note" title="Rules inside a mode live in the lobby">
Zombie Virus, Sky High, and the school set are names on a store page. Control lists and reward amounts are not in the paragraph. Read the lobby tip before you queue, and treat a “best character” guide as unverified. There is no class select in the sources used here. Customization is cosmetic.
</Callout>

## What you get

“What are Stars?” lists minigames among the ways to earn Stars, next to fishing, daily missions, and ad rewards. The Game Party section says you earn rewards and does not itemize them. Play one round and read the result screen. That number beats a copied table.

The go-kart frame above is store art. Steam’s mode list does not use the word kart. Treat the screenshot as a look at the driving minigames, not as a named mode entry.

School-only minigames require you to be at the school. The Camping Ground article places the School beside the Camping Ground on the Plaza side. Use the map pin labeled for the school in your client.
